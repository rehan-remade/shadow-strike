Click the game, then use the keys below

  • ←→ Move
  • SPACE Jump · again in the air for your class move (Teleport aims with the arrows)
  • ↓+SPACE Drop down
  • ↑ Climb · talk · portal
  • JKLU Skills
  • 12 Potions
  • ESC Help

Pick a class — Hero, Arch Mage, Bishop, Bowmaster or Night Lord — then talk to Elder Rowan by the campfire to begin. Every sprite, effect, song and sound was generated as code, baked into sprite sheets and WAVs, and rendered in Unity at a native 320×180 with a pixel-perfect upscale. Progress saves in this browser.
